Run the test. Send the report. Done.

Backflow Log is for licensed plumbers who test backflow preventers as independent contractors for public water systems. Enter your gauge readings at the device; the app computes pass or fail, captures your signature, and produces a Massachusetts-style test report PDF you can email to the water department before you leave the site.

A field workflow, not office software

From gauge to delivered report in one visit.

Backflow Log keeps the paperwork moving at the pace of the test, in mechanical rooms where there is often no signal at all.

1

Set up once

Enter your tester certification, plumber license, and test gauge. Add each facility you test, its public water system, and every assembly on site: RP, RPDA, DC, DCDA, PVB, or SVB.

2

Enter readings at the device

The form shows only the fields for that assembly type. Pass or fail is computed as you type, against the standard acceptance criteria. If it fails, record the repair and the final test on the same record.

3

Sign and send

Sign on the screen, generate a Massachusetts-style report PDF, and email it to the water department and the owner from the share sheet — before you drive away.

Built around the Massachusetts rules

The schedule and the report, handled.

Massachusetts cross-connection programs run on 310 CMR 22.22. Backflow Log's defaults follow it, and every device's schedule can be overridden for water systems that differ.

  • RP assemblies: semiannual retest tracking by default.
  • DC assemblies: annual retest tracking by default.
  • Failures: the 14-day repair-and-retest clock, on your dashboard.
  • Credentials: reminders before your tester certification expires and when your gauge calibration is over a year old.
  • History: every recorded test, searchable — useful evidence when your certification renewal asks for it.

Private by design

Your customers stay your customers.

There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or Good Little Apps data server. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Local records

Customers, devices, readings, and signatures are kept in the app's on-device store. Backflow Log does not upload them.

Deliberate sharing

Report PDFs are created locally. They leave the app only when you choose a destination in Apple's share sheet.

Nothing to breach

Backflow Log has no server-side copy of your business. We could not read, sell, or lose your records if we wanted to.

Your own backup

Export everything to one file and put it wherever you keep records — iCloud Drive, Dropbox, a folder on your Mac. It is your copy, not ours.

You can delete it

Delete records in the app at any time. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your Apple backup settings.

Availability

Currently in private testing.

Backflow Log is being field-tested with working Massachusetts testers through TestFlight before its public App Store release. When pricing is set, it will be flat and predictable — never a fee per test or per report.

Backflow Log is an independent recordkeeping tool for certified testers. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, MassDEP or any public water system. Report formats are modeled on the Massachusetts cross-connection program forms; confirm acceptance with each water system you file with, and rely on your certification training and the current regulations for test procedure.
